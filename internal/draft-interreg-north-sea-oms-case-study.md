## 1. Title

## 2. One-line summary

A long-running, multi-period online monitoring platform that underpins applications, evaluation, contracting, reporting and day-to-day programme operations for the Interreg North Sea Programme — designed, developed and maintained by Apogee since the late 2000s.

## 4. Challenge

Interreg North Sea is a transnational EU programme that funds cross-border cooperation across the North Sea and adjacent territories. Programme bodies, national authorities, controllers, auditors and hundreds of project partners must share a single, trustworthy view of applications, contracts, activity and finance — while meeting European Commission **e-cohesion** expectations (enter data once; reduce paper and duplicate reporting).

Over successive seven-year periods (IIIB → IVB → VB → VIB), rules, workflows and stakeholder roles evolve, but continuity of operations cannot. The programme needed a monitoring system that could:

- Support the full project lifecycle from idea / expression of interest through application, assessment, contracting, periodic reporting, changes and payments
- Serve many concurrent user roles with strict rights (Joint Secretariat, Monitoring Committee, controllers, audit authority, project and partner managers, authorised signatories)
- Scale securely across countries and organisations
- Stay maintainable and improvable as each new programme period begins

## 5. Solution — evolution across programme periods

Apogee has supported the programme’s digital operations since **2007**, spanning the IIIB, IVB, VB and VIB Interreg North Sea (Region) programmes. The Online Monitoring System (OMS) is the core operational platform for project handling.

### Timeline (Apogee + public corroboration)

## 6. What the OMS covers

Capabilities below are drawn from the programme’s public OMS documentation and Apogee’s published descriptions. Wording suitable for the site is framed as what the system enables, not as unattributed performance claims.

### Programme and project lifecycle

- **Applications** — expression of interest / application forms during open calls; draft, save, share and multi-editor collaboration
- **Assessment and approval** — support for programme evaluation and decision workflows (Apogee; expanded strongly from the VB generation)
- **Contracting** — e.g. project contract signing workflows documented by the programme
- **Periodic reporting** — activity reports and finance reports at partner and project (lead partner) level
- **Project changes** — change requests handled in-system
- **Controller designation** — designation of partner controllers for eligibility checks on expenditure
- **Refunds / payment-related finance flows** — refund transactions cited for the VB OMS expansion (Apogee)
- **Indicators** — dedicated indicator reporting module feeding activity reports
- **Document Centre** — structured upload and management of supporting files
- **Day-to-day communication** — in-system messaging/notifications between roles and with the Joint Secretariat

### Roles and governance (public programme description)

Typical project-side roles include:

- Project managing user  
- Partner managing user  
- Authorised Signatory (digital signature for submission of activity/finance reports and changes)  
- Partner controller  

Programme-side access (depending on rights) includes the Joint Secretariat, Monitoring Committee, controllers more broadly, and the audit authority. The OMS is designed to align with **e-cohesion**: data entered once and available to the authorities that need it, reducing duplicate paper submissions.

### VIB (OMS 3) emphasis

Relative to earlier versions, Apogee and the programme highlight:

- Unified interface for applications, periodic reporting and participation activity  
- New workflows for **partner collaboration**  
- **Digital signatures** for more secure, paper-light submission  
- Improved security and authentication  
- Redesigned, multi-device UI and broader feature set  
- Better data interoperability with the programme website and related systems

## 8. Why it matters

For EU programme authorities and public-sector buyers reviewing Apogee’s work:

1. **Mission-critical continuity** — Monitoring systems are not brochure sites. They carry applications, compliance, finance and audit trails across seven-year programme cycles. Apogee’s engagement spanning IIIB through VIB demonstrates long-horizon delivery and institutional trust.
2. **e-Cohesion in practice** — A single system of record for partners and authorities reduces administrative burden and duplicate evidence — a core Cohesion Policy digitalisation objective.
3. **Role-based multi-stakeholder design** — Controllers, authorised signatories, secretariats and committees need different rights on the same data. That is a specialised public-sector product problem, not a generic CMS install.
4. **Period-to-period evolution** — Winning successive tenders (notably the 2021–2027 OMS) shows ability to modernise (UI, security, digital signatures, interoperability) without abandoning operational continuity.
5. **Transferable Interreg expertise** — The same domain knowledge underpins Apogee’s wider Interreg portfolio (e.g. Interact INDEX, Interreg Europe Policy Learning Platform), which matters to buyers seeking vendors who already speak programme language.

### Title

Interreg North Sea Programme — Online Monitoring System (OMS)

### Summary

Since 2007, Apogee has supported the digital operations of the Interreg North Sea programmes. At the centre of that work is the Online Monitoring System (OMS): a secure, role-based platform through which project partners and programme authorities handle applications, reporting, changes and day-to-day coordination — now in its third major generation for the 2021–2027 period.

### At a glance

- **Client:** Interreg North Sea Programme (Joint Secretariat in Viborg, Denmark)  
- **Sector:** EU Interreg / public programme management  
- **Engagement:** 2007 – ongoing  
- **Technology:** Apogee Essence CMS — analysis, UX/UI, design and development  
- **Programme area:** Belgium, Denmark, France, Germany, Norway, Sweden and the Netherlands (~60 million citizens; €158 million co-financing for 2021–2027 projects)  
- **Scale (Apogee):** 6,000+ users across IVB / VB / VIB OMS platforms *[confirm]*  

### Challenge

Transnational Interreg programmes depend on a shared digital backbone. Applicants, lead partners, controllers, the Joint Secretariat, the Monitoring Committee and audit bodies must work from consistent data under e-cohesion rules — entering information once and avoiding parallel paper trails. Each new programme period brings updated procedures, while previous periods’ systems must remain operable for closure and legacy reporting. The North Sea Programme required a monitoring platform that could absorb that complexity without interrupting daily operations.

### Solution

Apogee designed and evolved the OMS across successive programme periods:

- **IVB (first OMS):** Built to monitor and manage complex procedures and internal communications for the North Sea Region Programme.  
- **VB (2014–2020):** Expanded to cover the majority of programme functions, including project application, evaluation and approval workflows, periodic reporting and refund-related transactions. The VB OMS remains available as a legacy system for the 2014–2020 period.  
- **VIB / OMS 3 (2021–2027):** Following a competitive tender, Apogee delivered a third-generation system with redesigned interfaces, stronger security and authentication, partner-collaboration workflows, digital signatures for submissions, and improved interoperability with the programme website and related systems. The live platform identifies itself as OMS 3.x by Apogee Information Systems.

Throughout, Apogee has combined product analysis, UX/UI, development and ongoing maintenance so the system can track regulatory and operational change across multi-year cycles.

### What the OMS covers

According to the programme’s public documentation, the OMS compiles project information and supports the vast majority of monitoring tasks. Project handling in the system includes, among other things:

- Project applications (including collaborative drafting during calls)  
- Periodic activity and finance reporting at partner and project level  
- Project changes  
- Controller designation and controller checks on expenditure  
- Indicator reporting  
- Document management via the Document Centre  
- In-system communication and notifications  
- Digital signature of submissions by Authorised Signatories  

Access is governed by role-based rights so that project partners, programme bodies, controllers and audit actors see what they are entitled to see — aligned with the European Commission’s e-cohesion approach.

### Impact

The OMS is not a peripheral tool: it is the operational interface for participation in the programme. Continuity from the first OMS through VB to the current OMS 3.x means the Joint Secretariat and project community have retained a familiar digital channel while gaining modern collaboration and signature workflows. Public programme figures provide context for the environment the system serves — including seven member countries, roughly sixty million citizens in the region, €158 million in co-financing for the current period, and dozens of approved projects already under way — while Apogee’s track record cites more than 6,000 OMS users across generations *[confirm definition before publish]*.

### Why it matters

Long-lived EU monitoring platforms demand domain fluency, rigorous access control and the capacity to renew a system without breaking trust. The Interreg North Sea OMS shows Apogee’s ability to deliver and steward that class of public-sector platform across multiple programme periods — the same discipline buyers need when selecting partners for Interreg and other Cohesion Policy digital systems.
